## Flagship case study in 60 seconds

**Project:** Synthetic hyperscale data-center development.  
**Schedule:** 808 activities; 1,406 relationships.  
**Cost baseline:** USD 120 million, simulated.  
**Baseline RFS:** 15 July 2027.  
**Reported updated forecast RFS:** 19 July 2027.

**Workflow:** Primavera P6 baseline → progress updates → cost and schedule analysis → EVM and forecasting → executive dashboard → management recommendations.

**Engineering insight:** design-release interfaces and long-lead electrical procurement require active monitoring because they can influence downstream energization, commissioning and handover readiness.

**Limitations:** synthetic schedule/cost inputs, modeled progress, externally calculated EVM and dashboard quality checks documented in the [methodology](./Hyperscale-Data-Center/docs/methodology.md).
