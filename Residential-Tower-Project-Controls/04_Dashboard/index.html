Approval gates

  1. Recalculate: align data dates, calendars, constraints and P6 settings.
  2. Verify critical logic: trace the driving path to handover and explain Recovery A's date shifts.
  3. Prove constructability: check crews, productivity, site access, inspections and safety.
  4. Quantify economics: assess acceleration cost and time–cost trade-offs.
  5. Authorize and monitor: approve only after evidence is reviewed; track weekly milestones.
