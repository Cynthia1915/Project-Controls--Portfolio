# Executive decision brief | Residential Tower recovery

**Decision requested:** Authorize detailed feasibility and cost validation of Recovery A as the preferred *candidate* schedule, while retaining Recovery B as a conditional resequencing option.

## 1. Position at update

- **Baseline finish:** 13 May 2028
- **Update 01 finish:** 31 May 2028
- **Variance:** 18 calendar days late
- **Update progress:** 7 completed, 3 in progress, 255 not started, from 265 activities
- **Schedule health:** 100 activities have nonpositive exported total float; this is a screening result, not a validated CPM critical path

## 2. Recovery alternatives

| | Recovery A | Recovery B |
|---|---|---|
| Modeled finish | 16 May 2028 | 18 May 2028 |
| Calendar days recovered | 15 | 13 |
| Residual delay | 3 | 5 |
| Primary intervention | Shorter durations for 4 activities | 3 FS handoffs changed to SS overlap plus 1 added FS dependency |
| Unchanged | Relationship set | Activity durations |
| Key uncertainty | Resources, productivity and acceleration cost | Safe sequencing, workface and handover interfaces |

**Relative benefit:** A forecasts completion 2 calendar days earlier than B. The difference does not establish A's commercial or operational superiority.

## 3. Management recommendation

**Gate 1 — validate schedule:** Confirm calendars, constraints, update data date, logic integrity, longest path and key milestone drivers in Primavera P6. Verify that the modeled finish changes are reproducible from the source schedule.

**Gate 2 — validate resources and construction methods:** For Recovery A, request crew/equipment plans, cycle-time assumptions, workface availability and inspection/curing constraints for EXC-030, EXC-040, TST-020 and TST-050. For Recovery B, prepare method statements for each overlapping predecessor/successor interface.

**Gate 3 — quantify trade-offs:** Build time–cost and risk comparisons; evaluate overtime, additional shifts, congestion, rework risk and quality/safety exposure. Do not calculate acceleration cost or ROI from absent inputs.

**Gate 4 — approve and monitor:** Only after site/contract review, approve a recovery baseline and track weekly milestone movement, critical/near-critical activities, resource capacity and emerging constraints.

## 4. Risk register for decision workshop

| Risk | Exposure | Proposed response |
|---|---|---|
| Compression productivity is overstated | A | Verify crew-based production rates and constraints |
| Concurrent workfaces interfere | B | Sequence handoffs and map access restrictions |
| Safety or quality hold points bypassed | A / B | Protect inspection, permit and safety gates |
| Schedule logic / calendars distort finish | A / B | Independent P6 network and calendar audit |
| Acceleration costs exceed benefits | A / B | Cost-load alternatives before authorization |

## 5. Evidence classification

**Verified from XER fields:** activity counts, status codes, relationship counts, stored project finish dates, duration differences, relationship changes and stored float screening.

**Engineering interpretation:** risk exposure, recommendation to evaluate A first and suggested decision gates.

**Not evidenced:** actual field productivity, resource sufficiency, cost, safety approval, contractual entitlement, independently recalculated CPM and a deployed dashboard.
